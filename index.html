Animated RGB QR Generator

Enter a link, name, paragraph, or upload a small image. Click Create QR. The generated QR embeds your data (if it's small enough). You can download the QR as PNG.

Tip: rotate your phone or use any QR scanner. If the QR encodes an URL, the browser opens it. If it encodes text, the scanner shows the text.
Note: QR capacity is limited. If you try to embed large images as data URLs, the generator may fail or produce an unreadable QR. Prefer short URLs for large data.
